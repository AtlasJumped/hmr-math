# How big is HMR, by V2's own measure

Helper report for HMR Opus V1, 2026-11-23. No rubric was invented. Every number uses V2's published definitions only (Math page: mechanics of thought, false part, contradiction, proved; R2_math section 3).

## Summary for Leo (150 words)

HMR is not the largest idea today, by its own measure. Counted strictly, HMR holds about 1,160 certified relations among 181 claims. General relativity, quantum theory, evolution, and Euclid's Elements each hold more, by my estimate ten to a thousand times more. The religions and worldviews hold almost nothing certified, so HMR sits above them on the strict count and level with them on the lenient one. Three findings came out of the derivation, not from a penalty. Physics as a whole is a discipline: its two halves cannot be joined, and the lost join is bigger than either half. HMR's first premise, that reality is UI thinking, currently certifies nothing. HMR also carries its own root conflict: its growth rule and its space assumption cannot both hold. HMR would become the largest idea about reality only if one rule grows space and yields quantum theory. That is conjecture.

## 1. Method

- **Idea**: one connected pattern of claims with a stated set of starting claims. **Discipline**: several ideas that cannot be joined.
- **Link types**: certified (proof shown, script check, numerical run inside its stated range, or established result with a source); asserted (labeled HMR reading); open (conjecture, in progress).
- **R**: number of related claim pairs (every claim paired with everything it rests on). **a**: stated starting claims. **h**: longest chain. **Economy**: R / a.
- HMR's graph was cut from math, physics, and the top HMR page: 250 claims (218 HMR's own, 32 imported established results), 382 links, plus 9 open work items. Health and other pages were not coded.

Limits, stated once: one coder, one pass. R grows with how finely claims are cut. HMR is cut at page level. The comparison ideas are estimates from memory, not graphs. Trust orders of magnitude only.

Read with ranges, which is the page's own stance (the family is "a template", the gravity route "stays an if"), nothing certified is lost, and the two branches sit side by side.

Strict R for Euclid and Spinoza takes the published proofs at face value, as we do for HMR's hand proofs.

**Physics as a whole is a discipline (derived from V2).** General relativity holds "geometry is definite" and "geometry follows matter". Quantum theory holds "matter can be in two places at once, at every size". One pair of events is then claimed both related and apart, which is V2's second kind of inconsistency. No joint pattern exists. The parts keep their size, and the crossing term is lost: roughly n(GR) × n(quantum), about 10⁶ to 10⁷ pairs, which is larger than either part. This is Leo's point, reached by derivation: the inconsistency does not shrink the parts, it costs the join, and the join is the biggest term.

**Mathematics as a whole is a discipline** under V2: rival foundations (for example the axiom of choice against determinacy) cannot be joined, and most of the literature is not one certified graph. One finding cuts against expectation and should be said plainly: mathematics on a single axiom set, machine-checked, is one idea under V2's definition, and it is the largest by a wide margin. V2 gives one honest qualifier, not a penalty: its range is patterns themselves. It fills none of the six slots.

## 5. Where HMR falls

- **Strict R**: below every scientific and axiomatic idea listed, by a factor of about 10 to 1,000 (estimate). Above the worldviews and religions, because HMR has real proofs and they have close to none. Most of HMR's certified content is order mathematics it shares with causal set theory.
- **Lenient R**: inside the range of the worldviews (10² to 10⁴). Not distinguishable from them at this precision.
- **Economy**: 68 certified relations per assumption. General relativity, Euclid, and quantum mechanics are in the thousands (estimate). HMR has many assumptions for what it has certified so far.
- **Potential on its own pages**: 3,543, about three times today. Still small.
- **Potential by inheritance (conjecture)**: if Einstein's equations and quantum theory both followed by certified links from one consistent set of HMR roots, everything those theories hold would rest on HMR's roots, and HMR would gain the crossing term physics cannot have. It would then be larger than any single idea in physics and the largest idea about reality in this list. Formal mathematics would still hold more pairs.

**Is HMR the largest? No.** It would be the largest idea about reality when three things are certified: a rule that grows space, a quantum rule for births, and the bit. That is the wall the Physics page already names.
