# The Holistic Model of Reality.
 Humans have always lived by models of reality: myths, religions, philosophies, sciences. Each explains part of the world well. None has explained all of it in one theory that holds together from start to finish. The Holistic Model of Reality (HMR) is that theory.
 A partial modelExplains one part of something and leaves the rest as given. A street map shows the roads and nothing of the buildings or the people. A photograph shows a building from one side only. A single instrument's part tells you the notes of that instrument and nothing of the piece. Each is useful, and each leaves most of the whole out.
 A holistic modelExplains everything it covers from the same starting point, so nothing is left as a loose end. A blueprint is a holistic model of a building: structure, rooms, wiring, and plumbing drawn together, each part fitting the others. A full orchestral score is a holistic model of a symphony: every instrument, in one key and one tempo.
 A holistic model of realityThe same idea with all of reality as the thing modeled: the origin of the universe, the laws of physics, life, the mind, health, and how to live. Materialism looks complete, because it claims everything is matter, but it cannot say where matter came from, why its laws exist, or why anyone is conscious. When a whole society shares a holistic model of reality, its people can agree on what is true, and everyone benefits.

The laws of physics are the stable rules that make a complex physical reality possible.  Seen this way, the open questions science leaves stop being anomalies. Non-local connection, the role of observation, and the existence of experience are what one would expect if intelligence comes first and matter second.
 We believe HMR is the most holistic model of reality yet put forward. It offers one narrative that runs from before the Big Bang to the present, accounts for science and for human consciousness, contradicts no established science, and finds a place for what the world's religions have described. Breadth is not proof. That is why, while we share it, we are also testing its most tangible part first: a new model of health.
